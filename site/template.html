What do Primary, Secondary and Tertiary mean?
Primary
Core ArcticGRO papers written by project personnel.
Secondary
Papers written by non-project personnel in collaboration with ArcticGRO scientists, and papers written by project personnel that take advantage of observatory data but are primarily associated with other projects.
Tertiary
Papers written by independent investigators that use the ArcticGRO dataset directly, use summarized results (e.g. average annual flux and tracer values) from the observatory's primary publications, and/or benefited from sampling opportunities provided by the observatory. Papers that simply cite ArcticGRO publications in their introduction or discussion are not included.
